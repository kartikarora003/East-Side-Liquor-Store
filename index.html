Good to know

Quick answers.

Common questions before you pull up to 4209 27 St.

Do I need ID?

Yes — we card. Please bring a valid government ID if you look under 40.

Can I call to check stock?

Absolutely. Call (250) 260-6606 and we’ll check the shelf for you.

Do you have cold beer and coolers?

Yes — cold case is stocked daily with beer, seltzers, and ready-to-drink options.

Is parking easy?

Street parking is usually available around the shop. Come by and we’ll help you load up.

Do you take cards?

Yes — we accept Amex, debit, credit, and Interac, plus cash at the counter.
